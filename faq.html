Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on quoting, files, materials, lead times and shipping. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Upload your CAD/STL/STEP files through our quote form with a short description of quantity, material and finish. Our engineers run a free DFM check and reply with a price within 24 hours.
For 3D printing we accept STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP. For CNC machining we prefer STEP, IGES or native CAD files (SolidWorks, Inventor, Fusion 360). You can also send a 2D PDF drawing with tolerances.
There is no minimum — we happily quote a single prototype. Volume discounts begin at 50 parts, and production pricing scales as quantities increase.
SLA/SLS/MJF typically hold ±0.05 mm to ±0.3%. CNC machining holds ±0.01 mm to ±0.05 mm depending on the part. Tighter tolerances are possible on request.
Standard lead times are 1–3 days for resin/FDM, 2–5 days for SLS/MJF, 5–10 days for metal and 3–8 days for CNC. We also offer a 48-hour expedited lane for many processes.
