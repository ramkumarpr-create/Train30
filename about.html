Our story

From a prototype lab to a full production factory

Azutva started in 2013 with a single SLA machine and a promise to never outsource quality. Today our facility runs seven 3D printing processes and a multi-axis CNC shop, serving customers across automotive, medical, aerospace and consumer electronics — from India to 40+ countries.

  • Engineer-led — every quote is reviewed by a manufacturing engineer, not an algorithm.
  • In-house QC — CMM inspection, material certification and full traceability.
  • Scale with you — same process and team from first prototype to 10,000 units.
